How to Play

Mission objective

Guide the blue Ion core and orange Solar core into their matching docking stations. Both cores must be docked at the same time to complete the mission, but they do not need to arrive on the same move. You can dock one first, then move the other into place, provided the first remains docked.

Gravity controls

Tap a direction to shift both cores simultaneously. They slide until a wall, the edge of the chamber, or the other core stops them. Docked cores can move away from their docks.

Phase Exchange

Beginning with Mission 8, Swap exchanges the positions of the cores once per mission. It counts as a move.

Phase Freeze booster

Use Phase Freeze to lock one selected core in position for your next gravity move; only the other core slides. The frozen core still blocks movement. One booster can be used per mission attempt. Start with one free booster, then earn another on every third distinct day you visit, with no penalty for skipped days. You can save unused boosters.

Scoring

Par is the shortest known unassisted solution. Many missions have more than one successful sequence. A booster-assisted completion earns stars, but its best score is tracked separately.

Helpful controls

Hint suggests a move from your current position. Undo returns to the previous state. Restart starts the mission over. There are no timers, lives, or forced ads.

No Standard Route Available

You can undo recent moves, restart this mission, or keep experimenting. Phase Freeze may open a different route if you have a booster available.

Return Rewards

New players get one free Phase Freeze booster to try whenever they wish. You also earn another booster every third distinct day you open Quantum Drift. Days do not need to be consecutive, and missing a day never resets your progress.

Your visits, boosters, and mission progress are saved in this browser only. They do not synchronize across devices or private browsing.

Audio Settings
